About STECIA

STECIA Digital Technologies is a Zambian registered company established in 2022. We are committed to providing comprehensive ICT and software solutions tailored to the unique needs of our clients, including software development, cloud-ready digital solutions, ICT support and hardware supply, so that businesses can thrive in an ever-evolving digital landscape.

We proudly serve schools, colleges, SMEs, NGOs and government departments. By understanding the specific challenges each client faces, we deliver relevant, innovative and effective solutions that help organizations maximize efficiency and grow sustainably.

Vision & mission

Vision. To be the trusted digital transformation partner for businesses and institutions across Zambia and beyond, through innovation.

Mission. To deliver reliable, scalable and affordable ICT solutions that enhance operational efficiency and service delivery for our clients.
